Changes: country CIDR groups were converted into sorted, non-overlapping IP
ranges, adjacent ranges for the same country were merged, and private, test,
and unspecified-country groups were omitted. Overlapping CIDRs resolve to the
most specific prefix; equally specific country conflicts fail the conversion.
Conversion script, code list and reproduction information accompany the Android
repair patch. These derived country ranges remain CC BY-SA 4.0.

A country is an approximate database assignment for the tested VPN server's
entry IP; it is not a guarantee of physical, exit, or user location. The app
looks it up locally and does not send the IP to a geolocation service.
